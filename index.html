Redefine Your Career Identity

OmniResume is an AI-driven career identity integration platform. We transform scattered digital footprints into a job seeker's most powerful professional weapon, and provide enterprises with the most precise talent insights.

The Problem

Highly Fragmented Data

Job seekers' achievements are scattered across LinkedIn, GitHub, various job boards, and personal cloud storage. For students, crucial records are locked within closed campus systems (like EECLASS, Moodle), making it difficult to effectively extract and inventory them.

High Customization Costs

When facing different job openings, candidates spend a significant amount of time manually restructuring their resumes to match the Job Description (JD). This results in many excellent talents being eliminated in the resume screening stage simply because they don't know how to "package" themselves.

Enterprise Evaluation Blind Spots

The resumes received by employers are often superficial and inconsistently formatted, making it difficult to quickly and structurally understand a candidate's true value and potential, leading to low recruitment efficiency and missed talent.

The Solution

OmniResume completely disrupts the traditional manual resume-filling model, creating a seamless experience through three core technologies:

  • One-Click Cross-Domain Aggregation (Auto-Aggregation): Through secure authorized APIs and an exclusive web crawler engine, it perfectly integrates campus systems with external professional platforms to automatically build a personal career Data Lake.
  • AI Intelligent Extraction and Dynamic Generation: Introduces LLMs to analyze keywords and skill requirements of target jobs, automatically selecting the most persuasive experiences from the user's database to generate a high-conversion, customized resume with one click.
  • Enterprise-Grade Talent Insight Reports: Provides recruiters with standardized, quantifiable candidate capability radar charts and fit analyses, transforming subjective text into objective data-driven decision support.

Business Model

We have built a dual-sided marketplace SaaS model with high gross margins and high scalability:

B2C: Job Seeker (Freemium Subscription)

Basic data aggregation features are permanently free. For high-frequency job seekers, we offer a $9.9/month Pro Plan, unlocking unlimited AI resume customization, deep interview question prediction, and career trajectory analysis.

B2B: Enterprise (SaaS + Matchmaking Fee)

We provide corporate HR with a $499/month talent search and analysis system. The system can automatically match hidden high-potential talents on the platform, charging a single headhunting matchmaking fee upon successful hiring, creating a highly profitable second growth curve.
