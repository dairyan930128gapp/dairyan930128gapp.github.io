Redefine Your Career Identity

OmniResume is an AI-driven career identity integration platform. We transform scattered digital footprints into your most powerful professional weapon, helping you land your dream job faster.

The Problem

Highly Fragmented Data

Your achievements are scattered across LinkedIn, GitHub, various job boards, and personal cloud storage. Crucial records are often locked within closed campus systems, making it difficult to showcase your full potential.

Time-Consuming Customization

When facing different job openings, you spend a significant amount of time manually restructuring your resume to match the Job Description (JD). Excellent talents are often eliminated simply because they don't know how to "package" themselves.

Enterprise Blind Spots

Employers receive superficial and inconsistently formatted resumes, making it difficult for them to see your true value and potential. We bridge this gap so your real skills shine through.

The Solution

OmniResume completely disrupts the traditional manual resume-filling model, creating a seamless experience through three core technologies:

  • One-Click Cross-Domain Aggregation: Through secure authorized APIs, we perfectly integrate campus systems with external professional platforms to automatically build your personal career Data Lake.
  • AI Intelligent Extraction: Our advanced AI analyzes keywords of your target jobs, automatically selecting the most persuasive experiences from your database to generate a highly-customized resume.
  • Standardized Talent Insights: We provide recruiters with quantifiable capability radar charts, transforming your experiences into objective, data-driven proof of your skills.

From Hours to Seconds

We are not providing just another "resume editor," but building your personal "career agent."

How It Works

1. Connect Data

Securely link your campus systems (EECLASS, Moodle), GitHub, LinkedIn, and portfolios. We gather all your achievements in one place.

2. Target Your Job

Paste the Job Description (JD) of the role you want. Our AI instantly analyzes the core skills and keywords the employer is looking for.

3. Generate & Apply

Get a perfectly tailored, high-conversion resume in seconds. Stand out to recruiters and bypass ATS filters with ease.
