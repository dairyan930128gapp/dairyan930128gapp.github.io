How It Works

We completely disrupt the traditional, painful manual resume-building process with three core technologies:

  • One-Click Auto-Aggregation: Connect academic systems (EECLASS, Moodle) and professional platforms with our secure API to sync your latest achievements.
  • AI Dynamic Generation: Paste a target job link, and our LLM will analyze the required skills and automatically select the most relevant experiences from your data to generate a highly-targeted resume.
  • Smart Interview Prep: Generate potential interview questions based on the intersection of your tailored resume and the target company's job description.
